# Local execution notes

These notes qualify the template, not the user's current instructions. Current user requests take priority.

- Working defaults are Seedance 2.5 at 480p for cinematic video generation; explicit user choices override them. These defaults do not force a sound-generation service, downsample approved masters or authorize spending. Confirm provider/mode support and ask before a materially more expensive fallback.
- Accept fixed CCTV, iPhone/phone video, handheld footage, archival film and edited references. For moving footage, account for pan/tilt/zoom/shake/parallax before interpreting motion; reset tracking across cuts. For archival material, preserve supported period details and mark unknown speed, frame rate, provenance and original colors rather than inventing them.
- Inspect the actual source. If it is a handheld balcony/phone recording, a news edit, or synthetic-looking material, describe it honestly rather than asserting it is fixed CCTV or authenticated footage. Source camera motion is not subject motion.
- The source clip's exact probed timing governs event checkpoints. **Beau's Seedance duration rule:** for a source S < 30 seconds, generate at least S + 1 seconds; for whole-second-only requests use ceil(S + 1), not ceil(S). Examples: 7 → 8, 14.5667 → 16, 29 → 30. An exactly 30-second original paired with a 30-second generation stays 30 → 30, with no extra second. With a 30-second provider cap and 29 < S < 30, trim the selected original to 29 seconds and generate 30, documenting the trim so the full one-second buffer is retained. Do not exceed provider limits or silently switch model/resolution. Preserve the generated master and trim matching intervals for assembly; the user permits trimming the original when needed. Do not delay, stretch or slow source actions to fill the buffer. A requested timecode is a target, not a guarantee of model synchronization; verify the output.
- Verified BytePlus Seedance 2.5 documentation: recommended prompt size is at most 1,000 English words (recommendation, not a stated hard limit). Supply a concise operational prompt plus an optional longer director brief. Direct API duration is integer-valued; video-editing mode instead requires duration=-1 and adaptive ratio, may output fractional seconds, and may shorten the input by about 0.4 seconds. Non-editing reference-to-video is the appropriate starting point for deliberately changing camera angles/aspect ratio; under Beau's duration rule request 19 seconds for a 17.2-second source (ceil(17.2 + 1)), then trim matching intervals without silently retiming actions. Docs: https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/modelark/create-video-generation-task-api?redirect=1 and https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/modelark/seedance-2-5 . Recheck at execution because platform implementations differ.
- Verify the selected provider's current model, duration, input-resolution, prompt-length and reference limits using first-party docs. Do not silently truncate the prompt, retime the source or split a one-pass sequence. Apply the source-plus-one-second duration rule above, including integer rounding and the exactly-30-second exception, before quoting cost or submitting. If the selected mode cannot accept a controllable buffered duration, report the limitation rather than silently dropping the buffer. Record both requested generation duration and final comparison duration, and disclose original/generation trims while preserving source-event timing.
- Mark observation, measurement, external reporting, creative interpretation and uncertainty separately. Sampling at 8 fps does not justify frame-exact peak claims. Inspect source frames at native fps when necessary.
- Template matching needs confidence/scale checks and visual verification. Reject occluded colour masks, lookalike shapes and out-of-frame landmarks. Image-plane offsets are not world-space distances without camera calibration; do not advertise them as measured physical gaps.
- Do not invent exact crowd totals, backgrounds outside the recorded view, identities, relationships, diagnoses, motives, causes, chemical smoke identity, confirmed destinations, later outcomes or verified sounds. Use the current source's visible evidence and explicit uncertainty.
- Publication dates do not establish filming dates. Independent reports may repeat one social upload and are not independent eyewitness confirmation. Separate corroborated context from attribution.
- Treat supplied safety/moderation guidance as a conservative content-selection rule. Do not obscure the provenance or reword content for the purpose of bypassing provider safeguards. Respect provider refusals.
- Reconcile contradictory template rules: sync/geography/identity precede camera authority; final 'still frame' means a steady live camera, not a freeze-frame; source-specific missing evidence takes precedence over a requested exact head count.
- A previously muted reference has no recoverable audio. Generated diegetic audio is an optional creative interpretation, not verified sound. Preserve a current user request for silent output.
- Preserve source aspect ratio in split screens. In particular, fitting a 1080x1200 source into a 1080x608 panel requires side padding or a revised layout, not stretching or another content-destroying crop.
- Installing the skill and delivering a prompt do not authorize paid generation, uploads of private footage, publishing or spending. Report what was actually generated versus only drafted.
